# Guide: Negotiating with Customer Stakeholders

FDEs negotiate constantly: scope, dates, priorities, and who gets what first.
The goal is a plan both sides can commit to, not winning the argument.

**What great looks like**
1. **Clarify the real constraint** — what's driving the date or the ask (a board
   demo, a contract, a budget cycle), and who actually decides.
2. **Lay out the options** — trade scope for date: "We can ship X by the 15th,
   or X+Y by the 30th." Give a choice, not a flat no.
3. **Name the risk of saying yes** — what breaks if you accept the unrealistic
   version (quality, a missed eval gate, burnout), stated plainly and early.
4. **Align, then confirm in writing** — get conflicting stakeholders in one room,
   tie the trade-off to their shared success metric, and recap what was agreed.

**Signals they want:** saying no while keeping trust; holding a boundary
without getting defensive; escalating cleanly when you can't resolve it yourself.

**Anti-patterns:** agreeing to the deadline then quietly missing it; negotiating
against your own team; vague commitments ("we'll try").
